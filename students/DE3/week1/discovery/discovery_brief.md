## What we are building
 We are building a product for lenders, banks, and procurement teams to run financial due diligence on companies, allowing everyday analysts to easily look up business records through a user-friendly dashboard without needing technical database skills.

## Who uses it
 clients of that company are from banks, lenders. They needed to perform a due diligence on the companies before lending money. 
 ## Questions it must answer
 It must verify whether a company is legally registered and identify its exact current status (such as Active or Strike Off). It needs to determine if a company has ever been involved in serious financial trouble or formal insolvency proceedings.

  Everything is clear, the entire worflow from data collection to the end product i.e a clean dashboard is fully understood.
 
 
## What "done" looks like.
 Single platform that contain all the company and the details such as whether the company is active, registered or is their any financial crisis, the history to  be kept, a clean dashboard that shows all the above information
